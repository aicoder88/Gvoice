Personal speech benchmark

Compare engines on recordings you choose and references you approve. Audio stays in your local corpus. Importing audio or results makes no model or network request.

How to build a useful corpus

Choose short real clips in Croatian, English and mixed speech. Include brand names, numbers, negation, self-corrections and realistic noise. Listen, enter the words that should have been transcribed, add protected terms, save, then approve each reference yourself. An English translation can help a reviewer with non-English clips; it is not scored.

There are no bundled recordings or pre-approved references. Run local Whisper after approving your WAV recordings, or import results from another engine. Local runs use your installed engine and model without downloading anything. Local CLI times include model loading and cannot be compared directly with warm streaming latency.

Engine and cleanup comparison

WER and CER measure normalized word and character edits, not meaning. Lower is better. Risk flags are imperfect prompts for review. Compare the same clip IDs and timing conditions. No engine is selected automatically.

Human meaning review

Read each output against the reference and listen when needed. Confirm whether meaning was preserved; pending reviews remain visible even when error rates look good.
